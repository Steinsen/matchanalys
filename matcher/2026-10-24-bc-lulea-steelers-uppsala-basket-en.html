Summary

Steelers led for 39 of 40 minutes, by as many as 19 (42–23 in Q2), but won by only one point. Uppsala won the fourth quarter 29–21 and got within one point three times: 85–84, 94–93 and 96–95 with eight seconds left.

15–6threes made, 45–18 points from distance
30–60points in the paint
11/21free throws for Steelers (52 %)

Three-point shooting kept Steelers ahead: 15 of 40 against Uppsala's 6 of 28, 27 more points from distance. Almost everything else went Uppsala's way. 16 offensive rebounds gave them 22 second-chance points to 12, Uppsala scored twice as many points in the paint, and Steelers missed 10 free throws. With 3.8 seconds left Kero missed both free throws at 96–95, but Uppsala took the rebound without getting a shot off. A week earlier Steelers beat the same team 104–82 at home.

Game flow

  • Q1 26–17Uppsala's only lead (3–2) was followed by a 12–0 run in 1:38: Erlandsson's two free throws, Jägare's three after Kero's offensive rebound, two E. Persson steals that led to his own layup and Kero's layup, and Enbom's fast-break three. Uppsala called a timeout at 9–3 and answered with 8–0 to 14–11. Aitomäki's three with ten seconds left made it 26–17.
  • Q2 25–30The lineup with Hjertstedt, Aitomäki and Sundelin won the first 3:52 of the quarter 16–9: two Kero threes, Hjertstedt's three, Sundelin's and-one and two Jägare layups (one after his own steal) made it 42–23. Then Uppsala went on a 10–0 run from 46–29 to 46–39, and Steelers had a 2:37 scoring drought. Six of those ten points came with both Jägare and Kero on the bench. Theodor Eriksson's fast-break three with three seconds left made it 51–47 at halftime.
  • Q3 24–19Jägare scored 15 of the team's 24 points in the quarter, including all 10 points from 55–50 to 65–52. Kero's turnover gave Uppsala a layup to 66–62, and Steelers called a timeout. Then came three threes in 1:23: Kero, Aitomäki and Jägare (on a fast break) to 75–66.
  • Q4 21–29Olsson's three after Erlandsson's offensive rebound made it 81–72. Uppsala cut in with drives and fast breaks, and after Jägare's offensive foul and another turnover, Häggkvist Sandell scored two layups to 85–84. Jägare's three, E. Persson's steal and layup and Kero's three made it 93–87 with 2:22 left. After that Steelers had no field goal and made 3 of 6 free throws, while Uppsala scored 8 points, 4 of them after two offensive rebounds.

  • Ball movement: 29 assists on 35 field goals (83 %). Kero had 9 and Enbom 6.

The problems

  • Rebounding: Uppsala grabbed 16 offensive rebounds (33 %) and scored 22 second-chance points. Two of them came in the last minute and were worth 4 points.
  • The paint: 60–30 in points there. Uppsala made 32 of 54 two-pointers, many on drives and fast breaks.
  • Free throws: 11 of 21 in a game won by one point. Forsman made 3 of 7.
  • Turnovers: 18 (20 % of possessions), 6 of them in Q4, which gave Uppsala 6 points. Uppsala had 12 steals.
