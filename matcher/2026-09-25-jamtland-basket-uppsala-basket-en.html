Glossary

  • Possession – a spell with the ball until the opponent gets it. Estimated as field-goal attempts − offensive rebounds + turnovers + 0.44 × free-throw attempts.
  • Points per possession – points divided by possessions. The best single measure of how well an offence or defence works.
  • Effective FG% – shooting percentage where a three counts as 1.5 shots, since it is worth 50% more points.
  • True shooting (TS%) – shooting percentage that also includes free throws. Shows how efficiently a player scores.
  • Offensive rebound rate – share of own misses the team rebounds itself.
  • Second-chance points – points after an offensive rebound.
  • Fast-break points – points in transition before the defence is set.
  • The paint – the painted area under the basket (the three-second area).
  • Scoring drought – the longest time a team went without scoring.
  • Run – a series of unanswered points for one team, for example 9–0.
  • +/- – the team's point difference while the player was on the floor. On/off compares time on the floor with time on the bench.
  • Lineup – the five players on the floor at the same time.
