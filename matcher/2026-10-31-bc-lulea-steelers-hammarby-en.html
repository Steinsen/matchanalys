Summary

Steelers won all four quarters and led for 36:28 of 40 minutes. Hammarby only led early (by as many as three, 1–4). They got within one (33–32) in the second quarter, but a 17–6 run built a lead that grew to 26 (102–76) in the fourth.

+0.24points per possession (1.27 vs 1.04)
16–7threes made (16/38 vs 7/20)

Six days after losing 85–97 in Stockholm, Steelers turned around the two numbers that decided that game: turnovers went from 24 to 18 and threes from 9 of 36 to 16 of 38. Hammarby stayed in touch at the free-throw line (36 attempts to 24, 26 made) and with 27 fast-break points to 18. Seven Steelers players scored 8 or more, and the bench outscored Hammarby's 50–32.

Game flow

  • Q1 29–23Causevic put Hammarby up 1–4 with two lay-ups. Jägare's three tied it 4–4, and Erlandsson's free throws after a Persson steal made it 6–4 – Steelers led for the rest of the game. Hammarby tied it 15–15 with threes from Taher and Kodu, but Erlandsson (three), Nilsson (three) and Enbom (three free throws and a three) made it 27–19. Six turnovers in the quarter.
  • Q2 31–23Cigoja's free throws made it 33–32 with 7:36 left. Steelers then went on a 17–6 run in 2:33: threes from Erlandsson, Nilsson, Kero and Enbom twice, and Persson's fast-break lay-up after a Kero steal made it 50–38. 13 of 22 from the field in the quarter, and Mugisha's lay-up with 27 seconds left made it 60–46 at half-time.
  • Q3 23–22The starting five opened with a 16–5 run to 76–51 (+25). Kero's two threes made it 63–46 and 66–47, Hammarby went 3:08 without scoring mid-quarter (66–49 to 71–51) and turned it over 8 times in the quarter. Over the last 4:28 Hammarby won 17–7 to 83–68, while Steelers made 3 of 11 threes in the quarter and Enbom missed three straight free throws.
  • Q4 31–23Aitomäki scored 8 points in the first 4:45 of the quarter, all on drives, and the five with Enbom, Mugisha, Nilsson and Olsson went 19–8 to 102–76. Steelers then went 2:47 without scoring while Hammarby ran off 10–0 (Lidberg 6) to 102–86. Jakobsson's lay-up, Kjällman's two threes and Curry's lay-up with two seconds left made it 114–91.

What worked

  • Ball movement: 28 assists on 40 made shots (70%). Kero had 7, Jägare 5, Enbom and Persson 4 each.
  • Three-point shooting: 16 of 38 (42.1%) from seven different shooters. Enbom made 4, Kero 3, and Jägare, Erlandsson, Nilsson and Kjällman 2 each.
  • Ball pressure: 11 steals, 6 of them by Persson, and Hammarby's 21 turnovers turned into 28 points. Kodu alone turned it over 7 times.
  • Offensive rebounds: 13 (33%) for 20 second-chance points to 13. Kero scored 8 second-chance points.

The problems

  • Fouls: 28 fouls gave Hammarby 36 free-throw attempts and 26 points. Aitomäki picked up 5 fouls in 12:52 and Jägare 4 in 17:12.
  • Transition defence: 27 fast-break points allowed, more than Steelers' 18.
  • Early turnovers: 6 in the first quarter, and Enbom had 6 in total. 18 is better than 24 in the last meeting but still 20% of possessions.
  • Focus with a big lead: from 76–51 Hammarby won the end of the third quarter 17–7, and from 102–76 they went on a 10–0 run while Steelers went 2:47 without scoring.
