Summary

Skallagrímur won the first half 52–33 and lost the second 30–51. They led for 34:13 of the 40 minutes and by as much as 21 (59–38 with 6:59 left in Q3). KV took the lead for good with one second left.

1.24 → 0.71points per possession, first half → second half
3 → 12turnovers, first half → second half
12–42points from threes (4/26 vs 14/37)

Skallagrímur won the inside game. They outscored KV 56–24 in the paint, out-rebounded them 50–38 and grabbed 19 offensive rebounds to KV's 8. KV made up the difference from the three-point line: 11 of 22 in the second half, after 3 of 15 in the first. Skallagrímur also turned the ball over 12 times after the break, which gave KV extra possessions while their shooting was hot.

Game flow

  • Q1 26–14Skallagrímur led 16–14 with 3:23 left, then closed the quarter on a 10–0 run, which became 13–0 early in Q2. KV went 4:22 without scoring.
  • Q2 26–19The lead stayed between 13 and 20 points. Markovic's put-back at the buzzer made it 52–33 at half-time. Skallagrímur made all 11 of their free throws in the first half.
  • Q3 13–28At 59–38 KV went on a 19–2 run, to 61–57. Skallagrímur went 3:33 without a point in that stretch and had 5 turnovers in the quarter, and KV made 5 threes.
  • Q4 17–23KV took their first lead at 67–65 with 8:34 left. With 5:24 left Skallagrímur led 74–71, then went 3:13 without scoring while KV ran 8–0. A 6–0 answer made it 80–79 with 1:13 left. Knudsen hit a three (82–80) and Markovic tied it with 26 seconds left. After a turnover at each end, Bragason scored the winning lay-up with one second left.
