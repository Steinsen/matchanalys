Summary

Luleå led for 38:59 of 40 minutes and by as many as 15. Arvidsson's free throws for 8–10 in the first quarter gave Luleå a lead it kept for the rest of the game. After 25–10, Norrköping got no closer than three (33–36 in the second quarter) and five (75–80 with 3:28 left).

Luleå won it with shooting: 43.8% from three against 27.8%, and 22 free-throw attempts to 9. Norrköping stayed in the game on the glass: 16 offensive rebounds, 17 second-chance points to 14 and a 40–30 edge in the paint. Five Luleå players scored 10 or more: Leach 21, Arvidsson 18, Naivalurua 14, Holmes 11 and Galette 10.

Game flow

  • Q1 28–16Tied 8–8 after 4:10, and Norrköping's only lead was 6–5. Arvidsson's two free throws put Luleå ahead for good. From 13–10 Luleå went on a 12–0 run to 25–10: Steinsen's three and mid-range jumper, Arvidsson's layup and three, and two Naivalurua free throws. Norrköping went 3:16 without scoring. Ninkingira's buzzer-beating three made it 16–28.
  • Q2 20–27Naivalurua scored 6 points in the first three minutes of the quarter (layup, tip-in and hook shot), and Leach's fast-break layup made it 25–36. Luleå then went 2:41 without a point while Norrköping ran off 8–0 to 33–36 (Taylor 4, Akuchie and Schüberg). Leach's three, Ninkingira's layup and Steinsen's three made it 39–46, and Arvidsson's layup with 9 seconds left gave 43–48 at the half.
  • Q3 18–13Galette opened with a three for 43–51. After Taylor's three, Luleå scored 7 straight to 46–58 while Norrköping went 3:32 without a point. Leach's three and Naivalurua's putback dunk made it 48–63, the biggest lead of the game. Odinks and Faas answered with threes, 56–66 after three quarters.
  • Q4 26–24Holmes scored 9 points in the first 2:45 of the quarter (a three, two free throws after a fast break, a layup), but Forsling answered with 6 points off offensive rebounds and an alley-oop. Threes from Hampton and Taylor, and Taylor's free throws after a Leach turnover, made it 75–80 with 3:28 left. Leach answered right away with a three, Naivalurua hit a jumper for 75–85, and Arvidsson's fast-break three with 1:12 left made it 78–89.

What worked

  • Three-point shooting: 14 of 32 (43.8%). Leach 5 of 9, Arvidsson 3 of 5, Steinsen 2 of 4 and Galette 2 of 6.
  • Three-point defence: Norrköping made 10 of 36. Outside Taylor and Hampton they went 5 of 24, and Schüberg and Smith shot 1 of 12 combined.
  • Getting to the line: 22 free-throw attempts to 9 and 22 fouls on Norrköping. Arvidsson drew 6 fouls without committing one.
  • Answers in the fourth: when Norrköping got within 7, 5 and 7, Luleå's next points were a Leach three, a Leach three, and Arvidsson's free throw and three.

The problems

  • Defensive rebounding: Norrköping grabbed 16 offensive rebounds (33%) and scored 17 second-chance points. Forsling alone had 5 in 17:50.
  • The paint: 40–30 in points close to the basket, including three alley-oops (Akuchie two, Forsling one).
  • Free throws: 16 of 22 (72.7%). Ninkingira missed both after a fast break in the third, and Arvidsson made 5 of 8.
  • The lead shrank: from 48–63 to 73–80 Norrköping won 25–17 in under ten minutes, and Luleå had a shot-clock violation with 2:28 left.
