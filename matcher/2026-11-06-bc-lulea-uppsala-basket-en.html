Summary

Luleå led for 39:48 of 40 minutes and never trailed. Uppsala got within one (40–39 and 42–41) in the second quarter. After that the margin was never smaller than 7, and it grew to 27 (102–75) in the fourth.

Luleå won on shot quality: 64.1% effective FG% to 43.0%, 22 of 27 free throws and only 8 turnovers. Uppsala stayed in it on the glass: 23 offensive rebounds (42% of their misses) gave them 23 second-chance points to 6. The third quarter was the only one Uppsala won (16–20). Daniel Hansson scored 16 on 6 shots off the bench, and Jonatan Arvidsson had 19.

Game flow

  • Q1 32–22Arvidsson opened with a lay-up and a three for 5–0. Rietz's three made it 18–15 with 4:23 left, and Luleå then went on a 14–0 run in 2:09: Leach's three, Hansson's three, Hansson's and-one, Naivalurua's fast break and Hansson's second three made it 32–15. Hansson scored 9 of the 14. Luleå made 11 of 15 shots in the quarter, Uppsala 9 of 27 – but Uppsala grabbed 11 offensive rebounds and closed on a 7–0 run in the last 2:14 (Swahn 4 off his own rebounds, Stümer's three).
  • Q2 27–22Uppsala's run continued to 32–24, and Swahn (dunk, three, tip-in) got them to 40–39 with 6:22 left, after a turnover gave Andersson a fast break. From 42–41 Luleå went 8–0: Galette's three, Steinsen's fast-break three and Naivalurua's dunk off Steinsen's pass made it 50–41 and forced an Uppsala time-out. Holmes scored 6 in 1:11 late (dunk, two free throws after his own rebound, lay-up), and Arvidsson's fast break with 13 seconds left made it 59–44. Uppsala went the last 4:23 without scoring.
  • Q3 16–20Leach's three made it 62–46. Luleå then shot 5 of 14 for the quarter, had 3 turnovers (one offensive goaltending) and gave Uppsala 8 free-throw attempts, including three shots on a three-point attempt. Olle Stümer's tip-in made it 66–55 and drew a Luleå time-out, and Swahn's steal and fast break made it 72–64. Uppsala were called for a bench technical with 2:31 left, and Galette's three off Hansson's pass with 1:01 left made it 75–64.
  • Q4 29–15Olle Stümer's free throws made it 75–68 with 8:57 left – the closest Uppsala got in the second half. Uppsala then went 3:11 without scoring while Luleå ran off 9–0: Arvidsson's lay-up and free throws, Leach's steal and lay-up and Naivalurua's three off Arvidsson's pass (84–68). Threes from Steinsen and Hansson made it 90–71. Uppsala shot 0 of 6 from three with 5 turnovers in the quarter; Luleå made 10 of 10 free throws. Galette's fast break made it 102–75 and Nilsson's lay-up with 17 seconds left 104–79.

What worked

  • Shot selection: 12 of 24 threes (50%) and 64.1% effective FG%. Hansson made 4 of 5, and Leach, Steinsen and Galette 2 each.
  • Transition: 26 fast-break points to 9. Hansson scored 10 of them, and 7 steals and 13 Uppsala turnovers produced 20 points.
  • Ball security: 8 turnovers (10% of possessions). Arvidsson played 30:42 without one.
  • Free throws and discipline: 22 of 27 (81%), including 10 of 10 in the fourth, and only 14 fouls to 21.

The problems

  • Defensive rebounding: Uppsala had 23 offensive rebounds, 42% of their misses, and 23 second-chance points to 6. 11 of those rebounds came in the first quarter.
  • Third quarter: 16–20. 5 of 14 shooting, 3 turnovers and 8 free-throw attempts allowed. The lead went from 15 to 11.
  • Scoring droughts after our own runs: after the 14–0 in the first quarter Luleå went 2:55 without scoring (Uppsala 9–0), and from late in the third into the fourth there was another 2:14 without a point.
  • Few offensive rebounds: 6 of our own (18%). Second-chance points finished 6 to 23.
