Summary

Umeå led for 35:22 of 45 minutes and by as many as 10 (38–28), but Luleå won 96–93. Galette's three with 24 seconds left tied it 81–81, and an 8–0 run in overtime made it 93–87.

Luleå won the game on turnovers and free throws. Umeå gave the ball away 20 times and Luleå scored 28 points off them, and Luleå made 12 more free throws. Umeå won the boards 52–36, took 14 offensive rebounds and won the paint 56–34. Four players scored 90 of Luleå's 96 points: Arvidsson 26, Galette 25, Leach 24 and Naivalurua 15.

Game flow

  • Q1 21–25Leach and Galette's fast-break three made it 0–5, and Luleå led 3–7 after Arvidsson's free throws. Umeå answered with a 9–2 run to 14–9 (Dunston Jr's free throws, Rask's layup and three, Gehrke's fadeaway). Abdulkader scored 8 off the bench late in the quarter, including threes for 18–13 and 23–16.
  • Q2 20–20Umeå opened 8–3 to 33–24, including Lundmark's tip-in after two of his own offensive rebounds. Dunston Jr's hook made it 38–28, the biggest lead of the game. Luleå answered with 7–0 in 1:05: Naivalurua's dunk, Arvidsson's and-one and Arvidsson's layup after his own steal made it 38–35. 45–41 at half-time.
  • Q3 24–23Isak Andersson scored four baskets in the quarter, and Umeå led 65–57 with 2:44 left. Luleå won the rest 8–3: Leach's three, two Arvidsson free throws and Arvidsson's pull-up three with four seconds left for 68–65.
  • Q4 16–13Leach's free throws made it 68–69, Luleå's first lead since 5–7 in Q1. Umeå went on a 7–0 run to 75–69. Leach made 6 of 7 free throws in the quarter and Galette's three made it 77–78. Then Luleå went 4:02 without a point (0 of 5 shots, four of them threes) and Umeå led 81–78. Andersson turned it over with 34 seconds left and Galette hit the three for 81–81 off Naivalurua's pass. Dunston Jr missed a drive with three seconds left.
  • OT 15–12Tied at 87–87. Then came 8–0 in 1:00: Naivalurua's two free throws after his own offensive rebound, Galette's three after Wollberg's turnover and Leach's and-one for 87–93. Arvidsson's drive made it 90–95 with 52 seconds left. Gehrke's putback cut it to 93–95, Naivalurua made 1 of 2 free throws, and Galette stole Dunston Jr's pass with three seconds left.
