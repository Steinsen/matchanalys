Summary

Steelers won all four quarters and led for 38:54 of 40 minutes. Rebounding (53–36) decided it: 17 offensive rebounds produced 17 second-chance points.

1.06points per possession, vs Västerås 0.93
53–36rebounds, 17–8 offensive

Västerås came back twice: from 45–35 to 48–45 early in Q3, and from 63–48 to 65–63 across the break into Q4. Both times Steelers answered straight away, the second time with a 16–4 run to 81–67 in which Kero scored 8 and Jägare 6. Västerås stayed in touch at the line: 25 free-throw attempts to Steelers' 4. It was the second meeting in a week; on 27 September Västerås won 104–94 in overtime, with 20 offensive rebounds and 45 free-throw attempts.

Game flow

  • Q1 26–20Västerås led 6–7 after three minutes; Kero put Steelers ahead 8–7 with two free throws and they led the rest of the game. Nasradin scored 11 of Västerås' 20 points in the quarter. Steelers led 21–13, Västerås answered with 5–0, and Olsson's three after two offensive rebounds plus Enbom's pull-up made it 26–20.
  • Q2 19–15Olsson hit two threes and Enbom one: 37–25 just over three minutes in. Västerås missed 3 of 4 free throws in the quarter. Kero's put-back made it 45–33. Half-time 45–35.
  • Q3 18–17With Persson, Löwgren Madeira and Erlandsson on the floor, Steelers lost the first 3:18 of the quarter 3–10 and Västerås closed to 48–45. After the timeout Steelers went on a 15–3 run, 7 of those points from Enbom, to 63–48 (+15) with 1:27 left.
  • Q4 25–23Västerås went 10–0 across the break to 63–58 and got within 65–63 with 6:03 left. Then 16–4: a Steinsen lay-up, two threes from Jägare and 8 straight points from Kero, with three assists from Steinsen. Västerås scored 9 of their 23 points in the quarter at the line.

What worked

  • Rebounding: 53–36 and 17 offensive rebounds (35% of own misses) to Västerås' 8. Kero had 17 rebounds, Jägare 5 offensive.
  • The defence on Nasradin: 6 of 20 shooting and 18 points, 11 of them in Q1. After the first quarter he was 2 of 11 from the field.
  • Ball movement: 23 assists on 35 baskets (66%). Steinsen had 10 of them.
  • Three-point shooting: 14 of 37 (38%) against Västerås' 10 of 35 (29%). Olsson 4 of 6 off the bench.

The problems

  • Shooting fouls: 11 fouls on shooters gave Västerås 21 of their 25 free-throw attempts and 17 points. Steelers only got 4 free-throw attempts on 81 shots.
  • Turnovers: 17 (21% of possessions), 9 of them bad passes. They turned into 17 points for Västerås, against Steelers' 9 off Västerås turnovers.
  • Starts of quarters: 3–10 at the start of Q3 and 0–5 at the start of Q4. The team's three longest scoring droughts (2:52–3:54) all came around the quarter breaks.

Glossary

  • Possession – a spell with the ball until the opponent gets it. Estimated as field-goal attempts − offensive rebounds + turnovers + 0.44 × free-throw attempts.
  • Points per possession – points divided by possessions. The best single measure of how well an offence or defence works.
  • Four factors – shooting (effective FG%), turnovers per possession, offensive rebound rate and free throws per shot. Together they explain most of points per possession.
  • Effective FG% – shooting percentage where a three counts as 1.5 shots, since it is worth 50% more points.
  • True shooting (TS%) – shooting percentage that also includes free throws. Shows how efficiently a player scores.
  • Turnovers per possession – share of possessions that end with the ball lost without a shot.
  • Offensive rebound rate – share of own misses the team rebounds itself.
  • Free throws per shot (FTM/FGA) – free throws made per field-goal attempt. Shows how often a team gets to the line.
  • Shooting foul – a foul on a player in the act of shooting. Gives two or three free throws, or one if the shot goes in.
  • Second-chance points – points after an offensive rebound.
  • Fast-break points – points in transition before the defence is set.
  • Points off turnovers – points scored on the possession right after the opponent loses the ball.
  • The paint – the painted area under the basket (the three-second area).
  • Scoring drought – the longest time a team went without scoring.
  • Run – a series of unanswered points for one team, for example 10–0.
  • +/- – the team's point difference while the player was on the floor. On/off compares time on the floor with time on the bench.
  • Lineup – the five players on the floor at the same time.
