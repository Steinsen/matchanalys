Summary

Nordväst made 41 of 68 shots (60%) and 33 of 45 twos, and won 109–102 even though Steelers took 14 more shots. Steelers led 24–16 with 2:30 left in the first quarter, then scored 3 points in 5:22 while Nordväst scored 18, and trailed for the rest of the game.

60–48points in the paint (Steelers–Nordväst)
8/35Steelers threes (23%), 3 of 17 after half-time
15–8offensive rebounds, 21–12 in second-chance points

Steelers won almost everything except the shooting: more shot attempts (82 to 68), fewer turnovers (12 to 15), more offensive rebounds and 24 of 27 free throws. But Nordväst scored 1.25 points per possession, the most any opponent has scored against Steelers in the analysed games this season (previous high 1.14 against Aros). The deficit peaked at 15 (48–63) early in the third quarter. Steelers cut it to 80–83 and 94–98 in the fourth, but got no closer.

Game flow

  • Q1 24–25The starting five went 15–9 in 4:40: Steinsen's and-one, Erlandsson's three, Enbom's three after an offensive rebound and Steinsen's steal and layup. Erlandsson's second three and Steinsen's fast break made it 22–14, and Mugisha's jumper 24–16 with 2:30 left, Steelers' biggest lead. Then Nordväst went on a 9–0 run to lead 24–25: Nasradin's layup, 5 free throws and Rusom's layup. Three turnovers in the last 71 seconds of the quarter.
  • Q2 22–32Nordväst opened with 9–2 in two minutes (Rusom's layup, Hagbert's three, Barlov's fast-break dunk, Hagbert's layup) to 26–34, and Steelers called time-out. Nordväst made 14 of 19 shots in the quarter and scored 20 points in the paint; Nasradin scored two layups straight off his own steals. Curry's and Enbom's threes kept it at 37–40, but Myron scored 7 points in one minute late in the quarter. Half-time 46–57.
  • Q3 23–20Threes by Barlov and Netz made it 48–63, Nordväst's biggest lead. Steelers answered with 8–0 in 1:58 (Erlandsson's layup, Enbom's layup after Kero's offensive rebound, Steinsen's fast break and Kero's tip-in) to 56–63. Nordväst made only 5 of 16 shots in the quarter, but Steelers made 1 of 8 threes and got no closer than six until Mugisha's three and free throw made it 69–73 with 1:25 left. Mugisha stole the ball right after but missed the dunk, and Hagbert and Nasradin scored 4 points for 69–77.
  • Q4 33–32Steinsen scored 13 points in the first 5:20 of the quarter, and the free throw after his steal made it 80–83. Barlov missed two free throws, but Nordväst got the rebound and Barlov's and-one made it 80–86. Rusom's three made it 91–98, Enbom's three 94–98 and Mugisha's layup 96–100 with 2:12 left. Then Nasradin blocked Mugisha and scored for 96–102, and Netz made it 96–104. Nordväst made 13 of 19 shots in the quarter but only 4 of 11 free throws.

The problems

  • Rim defence: Nordväst scored 60 points in the paint and made 33 of 45 twos (73%). 20 of their paint points came in the second quarter and 22 in the fourth.
  • Three-point shooting: 8 of 35 (23%). 43% of the shots were threes, and after half-time 3 of 17 went in. Kero and Steinsen 1 of 14 combined.
  • End of Q1 and start of Q2: 3–18 in 5:22, from 24–16 to 27–34. Three turnovers in the last 71 seconds of the first quarter.
  • Shooting allowed overall: 66.2% effective FG% against, the season high in the analysed games (previous high 57.4% against Aros).
