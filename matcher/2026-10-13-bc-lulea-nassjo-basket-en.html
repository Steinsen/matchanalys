Summary

The game was decided in the second and third quarters, which Luleå won 53–27. It was 74–43 after three quarters, and Luleå led by as many as 34.

Luleå led for 35:46 of the 40 minutes. Nässjö only led early (at most 0–3 and 7–9); Leach's three for 10–9 in Q1 put Luleå ahead for good. Through three quarters Luleå made 13 of 22 threes and Nässjö 2 of 15. Nässjö won Q4 by 14: they scored 18 of their 40 points in the paint in that quarter, while Luleå shot 5 of 14 and turned it over 5 times. The lead never dropped below 17 in Q4.

Game flow

  • Q1 21–16Nässjö led 0–3 and 7–9. Leach's three made it 10–9 and Galette's second three 13–9. Ninkingira scored 4 points late in the quarter, including a dunk off his own steal (17–12). Nässjö grabbed 5 offensive rebounds in the quarter but had 4 turnovers, two of them shot-clock violations.
  • Q2 28–14Luleå made 6 of 8 threes. Arvidsson's three opened the quarter (24–16), and from 26–20 an 8–0 run to 34–20 came on Arvidsson's layup and threes by Leach and Nilsson. After Galette's steal and layup it was 39–21. Leach and Arvidsson hit two threes 14 seconds apart for 47–25. Nässjö shot 4 of 13 from the field. 49–30 at halftime.
  • Q3 25–13Arvidsson, Leach, Nilsson, Galette and Naivalurua went from 51–32 to 62–35 in just under three minutes: two Leach threes and a Galette steal and layup. Nässjö missed all 6 of their threes in the quarter and had 5 turnovers. Holmes's three made it 72–42, and after a Naivalurua steal he hit two free throws for 74–42.
  • Q4 17–31Hansson's three made it 77–43 (+34). Nässjö then went on a 12–0 run in 2:33, to 77–55; Luleå called timeout at 77–50. Nässjö made 10 of 13 two-pointers in the quarter. Hansson scored all 9 of his points in the quarter (3 of 5 threes) and kept the gap around 20.
