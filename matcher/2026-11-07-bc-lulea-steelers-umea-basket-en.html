Summary

Steelers trailed by 25 (16–41) in the second quarter and 45–25 at half-time, won the second half 50–35 and tied it 70–70 with 3:20 left. From there it was 0 of 4 shots and 2 turnovers until 20 seconds remained, and Umeå closed it out at the free-throw line.

Two very different halves decided it. Before the break Steelers scored 25 points on roughly 41 possessions (0.60 per possession), with 8 of 32 from the field, 0 of 14 threes and 13 turnovers. After it they scored 50 on roughly 42 (1.18) and held Umeå to 0.84. Umeå still won at the line: 27 attempts against 15, and 10 of 14 in the fourth quarter. Steelers only led for the first 25 seconds (2–0).

Game flow

  • Q1 12–28Kero's fast-break floater made it 2–0, Steelers' only lead. From 4–6 Umeå went on an 11–0 run to 4–17 (Strömsten's three and layup, Nordvik's free throws and a layup off a steal) while Steelers went 4:22 without a point. 4 of 16 from the field, 0 of 7 threes and 7 turnovers, two of them shot-clock violations. Okeke-Nwakamma scored 4 second-chance points late in the quarter.
  • Q2 13–17Umeå opened with Okeke-Nwakamma's putback and Drottman's dunk off an Erlandsson turnover (12–32). Munthe's hook made it 16–41, Umeå's biggest lead (+25), and Steelers called time-out. Again 0 of 7 threes and 6 turnovers. The end was better: Enbom made three free throws after a foul on a three, and Jägare scored after Mugisha's offensive rebound, 25–43. Half-time 25–45.
  • Q3 25–20Mugisha answered threes from Drottman and Skogelid with two of his own (31–51). Steelers made 5 of 10 threes in the quarter and 10 of 17 shots, but 6 turnovers kept Umeå's lead between 14 and 23. A Jägare steal became Erlandsson's layup (47–61), and Kero's three after a Persson steal made it 50–65.
  • Q4 25–15Kero scored 7 points in the first 2:01 (57–65) and Mugisha's steal and layup made it 61–66. Umeå scored 5 points in 6:40 on 1 of 7 shooting with 5 turnovers, and Hjertstedt's three tied it 70–70 with 3:20 left. Then: Jägare's floater was blocked, Kero lost the ball for Strömsten's layup (70–72), Jägare's three-second violation led to two Strömsten free throws (70–74), and Jägare's floater and reverse layup did not fall. Drottman made 6 of 6 free throws in the last 33 seconds, and Enbom's three and layup made it 75–80.
