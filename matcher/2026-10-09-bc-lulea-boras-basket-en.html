Summary

The game was decided in the fourth quarter, which Luleå won 40–16. Luleå made 8 of 9 threes in the quarter, and Borås scored its first field goal with 1:17 left.

After three quarters Luleå led only 73–65. Borås led by as much as 7 (28–35) in the first half thanks to three-point shooting: 9 of 21 threes before the break. In the fourth quarter it turned completely. Borås shot 2 of 17 from the field and got 10 of its 16 points from the line, while Luleå scored 29 points in the first seven minutes of the quarter. Luleå led for 31:39 of 40 minutes and by as much as 34 (108–74). Borås took more offensive rebounds (14 vs 9) and scored 19 second-chance points; that kept them in the game until the fourth quarter.
