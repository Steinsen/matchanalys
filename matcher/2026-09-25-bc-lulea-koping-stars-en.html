Game flow

  • Q1 33–29Galette scored 13 points and Leach 11, both with three threes. Köping answered with Henderson's drives and offensive rebounds.
  • Q2 20–20Köping led 49–45. Nilsson's steal earned Naivalurua two free throws with 0.9 s left: 53–49 at half-time.
  • Q3 22–22Luleå reached +8 (68–60) after Arvidsson's steal and Leach's three. Köping closed the gap through offensive rebounds.
