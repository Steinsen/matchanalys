Summary

Luleå led the whole game from 2–0 and decided it by winning the ball. Högsbo committed 19 turnovers and Luleå scored 26 points off them. After three quarters it was 91–47, and the biggest lead was 49 points (98–49).

1.30points per possession, Högsbo 0.91
26–16points off turnovers
23–6second-chance points (13 offensive rebounds)

The game opened with a 10–0 run in two minutes, and three of the four baskets came straight off Högsbo turnovers. After 10–0, Högsbo never got closer than 8 points. Högsbo won the fourth quarter 24–17 after Luleå had gone to the bench: the lead dropped from 49 to 37, and Luleå had 6 of its 14 turnovers in that quarter.

Game flow

  • Q1 33–20Galette's tip-in after a Barton turnover, Arvidsson's fast-break layup and two Leach threes after steals made it 10–0 in 1:59, and Högsbo called a timeout. Högsbo came back to 28–20, but Naivalurua's three after an offensive rebound and two Arvidsson free throws with two seconds left made it 33–20.
  • Q2 21–10Högsbo scored 10 points and had a 4:46 scoring drought (from 39–27 to 51–30). Luleå went on a 12–0 run during the drought: Leach's jumper, three Galette layups (one after a steal), Holmes's layup and two Arvidsson baskets. Leach's three with 12 seconds left made it 54–30 at halftime.
  • Q3 37–17The deciding quarter. The starting five went 15–6 in the first 3:59, and Galette scored 12 points in the quarter, including an and-one for 69–36. Högsbo turned the ball over five times in the first 4:32. Luleå made four threes in the last 3:40 (Leach, Arvidsson, Naivalurua, Ninkingira) and the quarter ended 91–47.
  • Q4 17–24Ninkingira's two baskets made it 98–49, the biggest lead of the game. With Arvidsson and Leach on the bench for the whole quarter, Karras scored 10 points and Hill 11. Luleå had 6 turnovers and two scoring droughts of more than two minutes (100–54 to 102–59 and 107–61 to 108–66).

What worked

  • Defense that created offense: 10 steals and 19 Högsbo turnovers gave 26 points, and 19 fast-break points.
  • Offensive rebounds: 13 of them (38%) gave 23 second-chance points to Högsbo's 6. Naivalurua had 4 and Arvidsson 3.
  • The shooting: 29 of 43 two-pointers (67%) and 11 of 30 threes. Leach went 4 of 4.
  • Three-point defense: Högsbo made 4 of 23 threes (17%), and 2 of 13 in the first three quarters.

The problems

  • Fourth quarter: 17–24 and 6 turnovers. The lead fell from 49 (98–49) to 37.
  • Turnovers overall: 14, including 4 offensive fouls. Naivalurua had 5.
  • Fouls: 23 personal fouls. Holmes and Ninkingira both reached 5 and had to leave the game.
  • Högsbo's bench: 36 bench points to Luleå's 20. Hill and Karras scored 21 of them in the fourth quarter.

For the next game

  1. Keep hunting the ball. 10 steals and 26 points off turnovers decided the game. The same pressure on the ball handler at the start of each quarter.
  2. Fewer offensive fouls. 4 of the 14 turnovers were offensive fouls. Set the screen still and read the help defense before the drive.
  3. Fewer fouls. 23 fouls in total, and Holmes and Ninkingira both reached 5. Högsbo got 21 free-throw attempts, three of them on a foul on a three-point shot. Close out on the shooter with hands up without jumping into the shot.
  4. Keep the structure with the bench. Q4 ended 17–24 with 6 turnovers. Simple sets and safe passes even when the game is decided give better minutes to the players who get the chance.
