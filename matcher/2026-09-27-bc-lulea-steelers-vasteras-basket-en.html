Summary

Steelers led for 33:47 of 45 minutes, by as many as 11 (35–24), but lost overtime 8–18. Västerås won on rebounding and free throws, not on shooting.

8–18overtime (5 min)
11–20offensive rebounds

Shot quality was almost even: effective FG% 50.0% vs 52.7%, and both teams made 11 threes. Steelers had fewer turnovers (16 vs 18). The difference was that Västerås got 9 more offensive rebounds and 14 more free-throw attempts. Mir Nasradin scored 56 points, 22 of them from the line (31 attempts), and had 20 rebounds. In Q4 Steelers turned 66–72 into 83–79 with a 17–7 run, and Erik Persson tied it 86–86 with nine seconds left. It was the second game in two days, after Aros BBK the day before.

Game flow

  • Q1 24–17Steelers made 10 of 20 shots. Forsman, Erlandsson and Palmqvist had an 8–0 run to 8–2, and Palmqvist's second three made it 15–5. Västerås went 3:15 without scoring at the start. Persson scored 7 points in the last two minutes.
  • Q2 19–18Only 4 of 13 shots, but 9 of 11 free throws. Threes from Kero and Forsman gave the biggest lead of the game, 35–24. Half-time 43–35.
  • Q3 16–21Steelers went 2:51 without scoring across half-time (43–35 to 45–42) and had three turnovers in the first two minutes of the quarter. Kero answered with two threes to 53–46. Then 2:17 without scoring, and the quarter ended 59–56.
  • Q4 27–30Västerås went 8–0 across the break and led 61–59, then 72–66 with 5:48 left. Steelers answered with 17–7, 12 of them from Enbom, and led 83–79 with 1:30 left. Västerås went 7–0 (Nasradin 4, A. Nzonyansi a three). After Enbom's 1 of 2 free throws and Challans' 0 of 2, Persson tied it on a fast break from Erlandsson: 86–86 with 0:09 left.
  • OT 8–18Forsman had picked up his fifth foul with 0:25 left in regulation. Enbom gave Steelers an 88–86 lead, but at 89–90 Västerås went 7–0 in 50 seconds: an unsportsmanlike foul gave a free throw and the ball back (A. Nzonyansi scored on the third attempt after two offensive rebounds), then a three from Challans and a free throw after a technical foul on the Steelers bench. Steelers made 3 of 8 shots and 1 of 4 free throws in overtime, Västerås 8 of 11 free throws.
